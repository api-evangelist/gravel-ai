# Gravel AI

> Gravel AI is a beauty and personal care market intelligence platform that turns alternative data into early trend, ingredient, brand, and product signals for the global cosmetics industry. It provides analysis tools, curated datasets, market reports, and an enterprise data/API service that delivers licensed beauty data into customer pipelines (API, Snowflake, S3). Backed by Techstars.

## Use Cases

- Beauty brands: trend, competitor, and product intelligence.
- Ingredient and contract manufacturers: ingredient trends, OEM and supplier mapping, business development.
- Enterprise insights, R&D, procurement, and data teams: external beauty data for pipelines and data lakes.
